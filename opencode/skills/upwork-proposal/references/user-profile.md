## Proposal Tone Guidelines

From 12 analyzed Upwork conversations:

### DO:
- Start with "Hey there 👋" or "Hi there 👋"
- Lead with solution/approach, not credentials
- Use natural contractions ("I'll", "you're")
- Be specific about tech stack
- Ask for shorter version proactively

### DON'T:
- Oversell skills you're learning
- Use formal language ("I would be delighted")
- Include irrelevant skills
- Promise what you can't deliver

## Red Flags to Decline

Skip jobs that:
- Require skills you don't have (React-heavy frontend, DevOps)
- Pay significantly below market ($5/hour posts)
- Have vague scope with fixed price
- Request free work samples upfront
- Show client history of disputes/refunds
